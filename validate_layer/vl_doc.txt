Validation Layer (VL) Documentation
Current Revision: Draft 8 (Continuous Control over ETCS Level 2)

1. Why have a Validation Layer?
The RL Agent (PPO) is a non-deterministic black box.
This layer provides absolute mathematical determinism so the AI can undergo aggressive learning and exploration whilst ensuring zero risk of accidents.
PPO is used purely for optimisation; the VL is used for guaranteed, mathematically proven safety.

2. High-Level Architecture
This prototype focuses on a unidirectional convoy model.
It utilises Automatic Train Operation (ATO) Continuous Actions superimposed over standard ETCS Level 2 Fixed-Block infrastructure.

The L2 / Continuous Paradigm:
Environment Constraints (ETCS L2): The track is divided into fixed blocks marked by balises.
The environment tells the train the current signal aspect (0=Red, 1=Orange, 2=Flashing Green, 3=Green).
AI Action Space: The AI outputs a continuous floating-point throttle command: proposed_a ranging from -1.0 m/s² (Emergency Braking) to +0.5 m/s² (Maximum Acceleration).
O(1) Direct Interception: Because we possess a continuous physics model, the decremental safety sieve loop has been deleted.
The VL computes if the AI's requested proposed_a will breach the boundary allowed by the signal aspect.
If it fails, the VL deterministically applies Emergency Brakes in O(1) constant time.

3. The 5-Layer Safety Pipeline

Layer 1: Ingestion & Dynamic Limits
Inputs: x (current position), v (current velocity), proposed_a (AI continuous throttle), env_aspect (L2 signal 0-3), dtz (Distance to next zone boundary).
Logic: Maps the env_aspect to an exact physical clearance distance (distance_available = dtz + aspect * SH).
It then uses reversed SUVAT (v = sqrt(2ad)) to find the absolute maximum speed the train is physically allowed to travel before catastrophic failure.

Layer 2: Kinematic Simulation (Lookahead) and Multi-Factor Violation Check
Steps: The VL simulates the train's trajectory running exactly on the float proposed_a parameter over three 5-second intervals (T+5, T+10, T+15).
Justification: Simulating physics prevents the train from overshooting into a restricted speed zone or track boundary that is currently out of immediate range.
Spatial Violation: Ensures the 15-second projected position does not mathematically breach the distance_available track barrier established by Layer 1.
Temporal Violation: Ensures the train always maintains at least a 5.0-second reaction time buffer (TEMPORAL_BUFFER) before reaching the hazard boundary.
Segment Limit Violation: Ensures the train's projected speed does not exceed the static speed limit of whichever physical track segment it is projected to enter.

Layer 3: The Decision Node (Direct Interceptor)
Pass: If Layer 2 proves the floating-point trajectory is perfectly safe, return proposed_a to the train environment unchanged.
Fail (Instant Override): If the trajectory predicts a collision or speeding violation, throw away the AI's action and immediately return -1.0 (Emergency Braking). 

Layer 4: Explainable AI (XAI) Logging
Logging: Any time Layer 3 triggers a safety fallback, it appends a structural row to override_log.csv.
Data Logged: timestamp, original_float, corrected_float, constraint_id (e.g., Aspect_Spatial_Violation, S3_Limit).

5. Safety Constants
Max Acceleration (ACCEL): 0.5 m/s²
Service Deceleration (SERVICE_DECEL): -0.5 m/s² 
Emergency Deceleration (EMERGENCY_DECEL): -1.0 m/s² (Needs final verification from mechanical group).
Temporal Headway Buffer: 5.0 s
