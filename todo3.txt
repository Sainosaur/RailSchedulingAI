FIX 2 — Fix the progress reward scaling.

The current formula:
    out.r_progress = 10.0 * (p_current - p_previous)

produces values of approximately 0.02 per step because p is normalised
over a ~10km segment and the train moves ~20m per step. This is negligible
compared to the -0.5 step penalty and provides almost no useful signal.

Replace the progress reward calculation with a distance-based formula
that rewards raw metres travelled per step instead of normalised fraction:

    distance_travelled = state.current_position - state.previous_position
    if distance_travelled > 0:
        out.r_progress = min(2.0, distance_travelled * 0.02)
    else:
        out.r_progress = 0.0

Keep the lead train stall/hold suspension logic beneath it unchanged:
    if lead_train_stalled or lead_train_held:
        out.r_progress = max(0.0, out.r_progress)

Remove the span/p_current/p_previous normalisation variables entirely
as they are no longer used.
