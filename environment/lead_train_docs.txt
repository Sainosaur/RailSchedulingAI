RAIL SCHEDULING AI: LEAD TRAIN DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v1.0 (Line 104)

File: environment/lead_train.py

1. CORE CONCEPT
The Lead Train acts as the "pace car" and primary obstacle for the AI agent. 
It represents a preceding service on the line that follows a professional schedule but is subject 
to realistic physics, random delays, and external hazards. It operates autonomously within the `railway_env` 
but its physics are distinctly separated from the AI train's control loop.

2. PHYSICAL PARAMETERS
The lead train adheres to the following fixed performance characteristics:
- Acceleration (ACCEL): 0.5 m/s² (Standard traction).
- Service Deceleration (LEAD_SERVICE_DECEL): -0.5 m/s² (Comfortable braking for stations/limits).
- Emergency Deceleration (DECEL): -1.0 m/s² (Used during stalls/emergency stops).
- Dwell Range: 20s to 40s (Randomly assigned at every station stop).

3. MOVEMENT LOGIC (THE SPEED CEILING)
The lead train determines its target velocity (`v_ceil`) by choosing the most restrictive of four constraints:
1. Segment Limit: The maximum speed allowed by the current track segment from the Validation Layer.
2. Station Approach: A calculated speed limit using a reverse SUVAT projection to ensure the train reaches 
exactly 0 m/s at the next station platform.
3. Segment Transition: Anticipates upcoming speed limit drops, slowing down in advance to ensure the train 
is at the new limit before crossing the boundary.
4. Hazard Awareness: If a hazard (e.g., track defect) is active, the lead train calculates a stopping profile 
to halt before reaching it.

4. EVOLUTION & PHYSICS (ADVANCE)
The train advances using a two-phase SUVAT integration every 1.0s:
- Phase 1: Determines if it needs to accelerate, cruise, or brake to meet the current `v_ceil`.
- Phase 2: Updates position (`x`) and velocity (`v`) using constant acceleration formulas. 
If the train reaches its target velocity mid-step, the integration is split to prevent overshooting.

5. STATION MANAGEMENT
- Arrival Trigger: Activated when `x >= next_station_position`.
- Snapping: Upon arrival, the position is snapped exactly to the station coordinates and velocity is set to 0.0.
- Dwell State: A timer is initialized (20-40s). While dwelling, the train is "held," meaning it 
cannot move even if the timer reaches zero until the environment releases it.

6. TRAINING DYNAMICS (RANDOM STALLS)
To make the AI agent robust, the lead train has a "Training Mode" feature:
- Random Stalls: There is a 0.1% chance per second of a random mechanical failure.
- Recovery: Stalls last between 15s and 60s, forcing the lead train to emergency brake and wait, 
requiring the AI to react correctly to the closing headway.
- External Holds: The `held` flag can be set by the environment to simulate dispatching delays.
