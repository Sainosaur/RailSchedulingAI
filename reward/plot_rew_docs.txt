RAIL SCHEDULING AI: PLOT REWARD DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: v2.0 (Telemetry Plotting & Heuristic Audit)

The plot_reward.py script generates a high-fidelity visual audit of the 
environment's reward landscape across the entire 76.6km Line 104 journey.

------------------------------------------------------------------
A. OPERATIONAL MODES
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Model Mode (AI Analysis)
- Trigger: Existence of 'best_model.zip' in think_layer/models/.
- Logic: Loads the trained PPO agent and its Normalization statistics.
- Purpose: Evaluates actual AI behavior and policy performance.

2. Heuristic Mode (Stress Testing)
- Trigger: No model found.
- Logic: Runs a "Perfect Driver" dummy AI based on target velocities.
- Target Velocities:
    - Green (3): 100% of segment speed limit.
    - Double Yellow (2): 70% of segment speed limit.
    - Yellow (1): 40% of segment speed limit.
    - Red (0): 0.0 m/s (or 3.0 m/s crawl near stations).
- Purpose: Validates the reward function's "Golden Curve" baseline.

------------------------------------------------------------------
B. VISUALIZATION PANELS
------------------------------------------------------------------

Panel 1: Journey Profile
- Logic: Overlays Agent Speed, Speed Limit, and Lead Train Speed.
- Signal Secondary Axis: Shows real-time signal aspect (0-3).

Panel 2: Reward Components (Detailed Audit)
- Logic: Plots individual reward signals (r_progress, r_speed, etc.).
- Purpose: Identifies which components are driving the agent's behavior.
- Scale: Clamped to [-12, +12] to highlight continuous control signals.

Panel 3: Milestones & Punctuality
- Logic: Breaks out sparse event-based rewards (Stations, Time Bonuses).
- Purpose: Monitors subgoal achievement and schedule adherence.

Panel 4: The Golden Curve (Cumulative Reward)
- Logic: Simple integration of all rewards over time.
- Success Metric: A monotonic upward slope toward a terminal peak.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C. TECHNICAL CLAUSE: EXPLAINING THE TERMINAL DIP
------------------------------------------------------------------

Observation: In Heuristic Mode, the Golden Curve shows a sharp -1000 dip 
at the very end of the journey (76.6km).

1. The Lead Train Constraint:
   The lead train parks at 76,685.7m (roughly 35m past the terminus).

2. Heuristic Arrival shortfall:
   The dummy AI heuristic stops at 76,594m (approximately 57m short of 
   the actual TRACK_END).

3. State Interlock:
   At 76,594m, the distance to the lead train zone start is exactly 
   1 SH (40m), which forces train_aspect to RED (0).

4. Stagnation Period:
   Because TRACK_END termination was removed and MAX_STEPS=10000 is 
   high, the dummy AI correctly obeys the Red signal and sits stationary 
   at the 76.5km mark for the remaining simulation steps.

5. Step Penalty Bleed:
   During this "idling" period, the agent continues to incur the 
   -0.5 r_step penalty on every timestep.

Result: 2,000 steps of idling × -0.5 = -1,000 point dip. 
Note: The real PPO agent will not exhibit this behavior as it is 
trained to maximize progress and will naturally drive to the terminus 
to stop the step penalty accumulation.
