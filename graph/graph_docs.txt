RAIL SCHEDULING AI: TRACK GRAPH & INFRASTRUCTURE DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v2.0 (Line 104)

File: graph/graph.py

1. CORE CONCEPT
The Graph module provides a structured, graph-based representation of the 76km track, including station coordinates, elevations, gradients, and fixed-block boundaries.

2. DATA MODELS

A. Station
Represents a physical terminal or stop.
- distance (km): Kilometrage along the line.
- elevation (m): Height above sea level.
- passing_loop: Boolean stating if the station allows for overtaking.

B. Segment & VLSegment
Represents the track between two stations.
- speed_limit (km/h): The maximum permitted line speed.
- VLSegment: A specialized version used by the Validation Layer, featuring speed in m/s and absolute track meterage.

C. Block
Represents a sub-division of a Segment. Used for the fixed-block signaling simulation.

3. THE HEADWAY TABLE (SH/TH)
The track is divided into segments with varying spatial (SH) and temporal (TH) headways:
- S0 & S1: SH = 390.0m, TH = 15.6s.
- S2: SH = 170.0m, TH = 10.2s.
- S3, S4, S5: SH = 40.0m, TH = 4.8s.

4. ARITHMETIC ZONE ALLOCATION
In this revision, zone boundaries are no longer pre-computed into lists. Instead, the Validation Layer (VL) calculates zone boundaries arithmetically based on the `spatial_headway` (SH) and segment boundaries.
- The last zone of each segment is the station stopping zone.
- Safety Merge: The arithmetic logic ensures that any fractional remainder at the end of a segment is absorbed into the final zone, making it oversized (>= SH).

5. GEOGRAPHIC CONSTANTS
- Elevations: Hardcoded from SRTM30m data for Line 104 (Chabówka 553.0m to Nowy Sącz 292.0m).
- Distances: Precise kilometer markers (e.g., Nowy Sącz at 76.651 km).

6. CACHING & PERFORMANCE
The graph and VLSegments are computed lazily and cached to minimize overhead. Segment lookup is O(n), while zone arithmetic within a segment is O(1).
