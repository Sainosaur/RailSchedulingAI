RAIL SCHEDULING AI: GYMNASIUM ENVIRONMENT DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v1.0 (Synchronized)

File: environment/railway_env.py

1. CORE CONCEPT (THE CONVOY MODEL)
The environment simulates a single AI-controlled train traversing the 76km Polish Rail Line 104, interacting with a persistent lead train.
- Lead Train: Spawns 2,000m ahead of the AI. It follows a realistic schedule, dwelling for 20-40s at every station and adhering to segment speed limits and signal aspects.
- The AI Train: Guided by the RL model, it must maintain a smooth, punctual, and safe trajectory from Chabówka to Nowy Sącz.

2. AGENT COMMUNICATION (SPACES)

A. Observation Space (What the AI Sees)
A 7-dimensional Box space (array of floats) providing real-time telemetry every second:
- [0] Position: Current meters traveled along the 76,651m track.
- [1] Speed: Current velocity (m/s).
- [2] DTZ (Distance-To-Zone): Distance to the next fixed-block signal boundary (m).
- [3] Signal Aspect: 0=Red, 1=Yellow, 2=Double Yellow, 3=Green.
- [4] Distance to Occupied: Physical distance to the nearest obstruction (lead train or hazard).
- [5] Lead Train Speed: Current velocity of the train ahead (m/s).
- [6] Sim Time: Elapsed time in seconds since the start of the journey.

B. Action Space (What the AI Does)
A continuous Box space [-1.0, 1.0].
- Remapping: The environment maps [0, 1] to [0.0, 0.5] (Acceleration) and [-1, 0] to [-1.0, 0.0] (Deceleration).
- Safety Intercept: The Validation Layer (VL) intercepts the proposed action. If unsafe, it is overridden (typically to emergency braking).

3. THE SIMULATION STEP (1.0s DT)

PHASE 1: Validation & Interlock
The VL evaluates the action using an adaptive kinematic lookahead (Projecting for the duration of a full stopping distance). 
- Red Signal Interlock: If speed < 0.1 and signal is Red, acceleration is forced to 0.0.
- Speed Limit Governor: If at the speed limit, acceleration is capped to 0.0 or lower.

PHASE 2: SUVAT Physics
Applies the (potentially overridden) acceleration using a two-phase check:
- Boundary Awareness: If the train would hit 0 m/s or the Speed Limit mid-step, the environment calculates the exact fractional second of movement and caps the velocity for the remainder of the step.

PHASE 3: Lead Train Advancement
The Lead Train (`lead_train.py`) advances through Dwell, Braking, and Cruise states. The AI must manage its own headway to avoid collisions or critical safety violations.

PHASE 4: Station Management
- Detection: Arrival is triggered at Position >= (Station_Pos - 2.0m).
- Dwell Logic: Upon arrival, a random dwell period (20-40s) is assigned. The signal aspect is forced to RED (0) during this period.
- Departure: Position tracking resumes only after the dwell timer expires.

PHASE 5: Reward Synthesis
Live telemetry is passed to `reward_function.py`. The "Golden Curve" reward is composed of:
- Dense Metrics: Progress per step, Speed Envelope compliance, Signal "Sweet Spot" bonuses.
- Sparse Events: Station arrival milestones (escalating), Punctuality bonuses (+10k), and Jerk/Override penalties.

PHASE 6: Termination & Truncation
- Termination: Occurs at Track End (76,651m), Collision, Critical Headway Violation (<1x TH), or exceeding the 20-minute Lateness Threshold.
- Truncation: A hard cap of 10,000 steps (~2.8 hours) prevents hung training sessions.

4. TELEMETRY (Info Dictionary)
On every step, the environment returns an 'info' dictionary for debugging:
- current_position / current_speed: Raw physical data.
- safety_overridden: Boolean flag for VL intervention.
- aspect / segment: Current track-side telemetry.
- reward_breakdown: Granular breakdown (Progress, Speed, Lateness, etc.).
- punctuality_status: ETA vs Scheduled Arrival for the next station.
