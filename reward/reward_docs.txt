RAIL SCHEDULING AI: REWARD & PENALTY DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v1.0 (Synchronized)

The total reward (r_total) is calculated every timestep as:
r_total = r_continuous + r_event + r_terminal

------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CONTINUOUS REWARDS (Calculated every physical timestep)
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Progress Reward (_compute_progress_reward)
- Goal: Incentivize forward movement towards the goal.
- Logic: Rewards based on the change in percentage progress between the last station and the next station.
- Scale: k_p = 2000.0 (High density signal).

2. Speed Target & Braking Envelope (_compute_speed_reward)
- Goal: Create smooth, ETCS-compliant velocity curves.
- Logic: Targets a physics-driven "Perfect Speed" based on the track limit and a proactive braking curve (v = sqrt(2ad)) when approaching stations or obstacles.
- Penalty: Linear penalties for overspeeding (k_over) and underspeeding (k_under).

3. Cruise & Standing Bonus (_compute_cruise_reward)
- Goal: Maintain stability at high speeds.
- Reward: Bonus for maintaining 90% to 105% of the Speed Limit.
- Efficiency: Additional +2.0 bonus for "Active Coasting" (near-zero acceleration at high speed).

4. Signal Compliance & "Sweet Spot" (_compute_signal_compliance_reward)
- Goal: Professional interaction with the signal wave.
- Sweet Spot: High bonus for staying 3-4 blocks behind the lead train (maintaining the "Green Wave").
- Yellow Tax: Small penalty for "hanging out" in restricted (Yellow/Double Yellow) signals while moving.
- Red Punishment: Massive penalty (-5000) for any attempt to accelerate from a stop at a Red signal.

5. Headway Penalty (_compute_headway_penalty)
- Goal: Maintain safe spacing without triggering terminal violations.
- Logic: A linear ramp penalty that activates at 3x Temporal Headway and scales to -k_h as the train approaches the critical violation boundary.

6. Patience & Stall Logic (_compute_patience_reward / _compute_heartbeat_penalty)
- Patience: Positive reward (+10.0) for remaining perfectly stationary during mandatory Dwells or at Red signals.
- Stalling: Severe penalty (-40.0) for being stationary at a Green signal when the road is clear.
- Existence Penalty: Constant -0.1 per step to encourage the AI to eventually finish the episode.

------------------------------------------------------------------
B. EVENT REWARDS (Triggered on specific conditions)
------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Station Milestones (_compute_station_reward)
- Logic: Positive reward granted upon arrival at the station border.
- Escalation: Rewards increase by 30% for each subsequent station to pull the agent through the whole route.
- Grand Prize: A massive +50,000 bonus for reaching the final terminal (Nowy Sącz).

8. Punctuality & Schedule (_compute_punctuality_penalty)
- Carrot: A massive +10,000 bonus for arriving within a 60-second window of the timetable.
- Stick: Scaling deduction for arriving early or late outside that tolerance window.

9. Override Penalty (_compute_override_penalty)
- Goal: Train the AI to be "Autonomous Ready."
- Penalty: Violently punishes the model (-100.0) whenever the Validation Layer (VL) has to forcefully override an unsafe action.

10. Operational Jerk & Energy (_compute_jerk_penalty / _compute_energy_penalty)
- Jerk: Penalizes the square of the acceleration delta to prevent whiplash-inducing control changes.
- Energy: (Optional) Penalizes the magnitude of traction applications.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C. TERMINAL PENALTIES (Triggers "Terminate = True")
------------------------------------------------------------------

11. Collision & Headway Violation
- Penalty: -100,000 (Fatal). Episode ends immediately in total failure if the train collides with the lead train or crosses the critical safety headway.

12. Lateness Threshold
- Penalty: -10,000 (Failure). Episode ends if the train fails to reach the next station within 20 minutes (1200s) of its scheduled arrival time. This prevents the AI from "parking" on the track to avoid other penalties.
