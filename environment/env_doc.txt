RAIL SCHEDULING AI: GYMNASIUM ENVIRONMENT DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v2.0 (Arithmetic VL & Direct Control)

File: environment/railway_env.py

1. CORE CONCEPT (THE CONVOY MODEL)
The environment simulates a single AI-controlled train traversing the 76km Polish Rail Line 104, interacting with a persistent lead train.
- Lead Train: Spawns 2,000m ahead of the AI. It follows a realistic schedule, dwelling for 20-40s at every station and adhering to segment speed limits and signal aspects.
- The AI Train: Guided by the RL model, it must maintain a smooth, punctual, and safe trajectory from Chabówka to Nowy Sącz.

2. AGENT COMMUNICATION (SPACES)

A. Observation Space (What the AI Sees)
A 9-dimensional Box space (array of floats) providing relative, bounded telemetry [0, 1] every second:
- [0] progress: How far through the current inter-station segment (0.0 to 1.0).
- [1] v_ratio: Current speed relative to segment speed limit (0.0 to 1.5).
- [2] dtz_norm: Distance to end of zone, normalized by Spatial Headway (0.0 to 5.0).
- [3] train_aspect: Signal aspect relative to lead train (0=Red, 0.33=Yellow, 0.66=DY, 1.0=Green).
- [4] station_aspect: Signal aspect relative to next station (0=Red, 1.0=Green).
- [5] gap_sh: Headway gap to the lead train in SH units, normalized to max 20 (0.0 to 1.0).
- [6] next_limit_ratio: Lookahead speed limit of the upcoming segment (0.0 to 1.0).
- [7] dist_to_st: Distance to the next station normalized by total route length (0.0 to 1.0).
- [8] time_frac: Episode step count over MAX_STEPS (0.0 to 1.0).

B. Action Space (What the AI Does)
A continuous Box space [-1.0, 1.0].
- No Remapping: The raw action value IS the proposed acceleration directly.
  Valid range: [0.0, 0.5] for acceleration, [-1.0, 0.0] for deceleration.
  Any action outside these bounds terminates the episode immediately.
- Direct Control: The Validation Layer (VL) no longer overrides actions. It only logs violations.
- Physics Termination: If proposed_a > 0.5 or < -1.0, the episode terminates immediately.

3. THE SIMULATION STEP (1.0s DT)

PHASE 1: Physics (SUVAT)
Applies the proposed acceleration using standard equations of motion.
- Negative Speed Guard: If the resulting speed would be negative, the episode terminates.

PHASE 2: Validation & Logging
The VL evaluates the state after movement:
- Log-Only: Violations are written to `override_log.csv` but the action is never altered.
- Constraint IDs: SPATIAL_VIOLATION, SPEED_LIMIT_VIOLATION, STATION_SIGNAL_VIOLATION, etc.

PHASE 3: Lead Train Advancement
The Lead Train (`lead_train.py`) advances through Dwell, Braking, and Cruise states.

PHASE 4: Station Management
- Detection: Arrival is triggered when `x >= x_station_zone_start` (the start of the last zone of the current segment).
- Dwell Logic: Upon arrival, a random dwell period (20-40s) is assigned. `station_cleared` is set to False.
- Departure: `station_cleared` becomes True after the dwell timer expires.

PHASE 5: Reward Synthesis
Live telemetry and violation flags are passed to `reward_function.py`.
- Dense Metrics: Progress, Speed Compliance (relative to optimal braking), Regenerative Braking rewards.
- Sparse Events: Station milestones, Punctuality bonuses.

PHASE 6: Termination & Truncation
- Termination: Track End, Collision (zone overlap, x_diff <= 0), Negative Speed, or Impossible Action.
- Truncation: A hard cap of 10,000 steps (~2.8 hours).

4. TELEMETRY (Info Dictionary)
On every step, the environment returns an 'info' dictionary for debugging:
- train_aspect / station_aspect: Signal state.
- station_cleared: Dwell status.
- x_ai_zone_end / x_lead_zone_start / x_station_zone_start: Zone boundaries.
- optimal_braking_distance: Calculated safety threshold.
- violations: Dictionary of violation flags returned by the VL.
