RAIL SCHEDULING AI: REWARD & PENALTY DOCUMENTATION
Current Revision: Final Prototype v2.0 (Arithmetic VL & Direct Control)

The total reward (r_total) is calculated every timestep as a weighted sum:
r_total = sum of all active components.

------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CONTINUOUS REWARDS (Calculated every physical timestep)
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Progress Reward
- Goal: Incentivize forward movement.
- Logic: Change in percentage progress between stations.
- Lead Protection: Suspend accumulation if the lead train is stalled or held.

2. Speed Compliance (Aspect-Based)
- Goal: Smooth, safe velocity curves driven by signal aspect.
- Regime uses effective_aspect = min(train_aspect, station_aspect).
- Green (3): Penalty proportional to deviation from the segment speed limit.
- Double Yellow / Yellow (2/1): Reward (+5.0) for service braking at 0.5 m/s².
  Penalty (-2.0) for braking harder than 0.5 m/s² but not emergency.
  Emergency braking: see rule below.
- Red (0): Reward (+2.0) for braking toward stop. No reward if already stopped.
  Penalty (-5.0) for moving at Red without adequate braking.
  Emergency braking: see rule below.
- Emergency Braking Rule: Emergency braking (a <= -1.0 m/s²) is only justified
  when effective_aspect == 0 (Red) AND speed >= 80% of the segment speed limit.
  In all other contexts, emergency braking incurs a penalty (-10.0).

3. Regenerative Braking
- Goal: Energy efficiency.
- Reward: +2.0 per step if deceleration is between -0.5 and -0.01 m/s².
- Penalty: Small penalty for non-regenerative deceleration between -1.0 and -0.5 m/s².

4. Jerk Compliance
- Goal: Passenger comfort.
- Reward: +1.0 per step if jerk < 0.5 m/s³/s.
- Penalty: Proportional to (jerk - 1.0) if jerk > 1.0.

5. Signal Compliance
- Lead Train Signal: Massive penalty for accelerating at a Red train aspect. Sweet spot bonus (+5.0) for staying 3-4 SH behind the lead train, provided the lead train is actively moving (v > 0.5) and the absolute gap is less than 8 SH.
- Station Signal: Penalty for approaching station zone at speed during dwell.

6. Headway Penalty
- Logic: Linear ramp penalty activating when x_diff < 3*SH.
- x_diff = x_lead_zone_start - x_ai_zone_end.
- Scale: 0 at 3*SH to -k_h at 1*SH.

7. Patience & Stall Logic
- Patience: +10.0 per step while stationary during mandatory dwell.
- Stalling: -20.0 per step if stationary at a Green signal with a clear road.
- Existence: -0.1 per step tax.

------------------------------------------------------------------
B. EVENT REWARDS (Triggered on specific conditions)
------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Station Milestones
- Rewards increase by 30% per station.
- Grand Prize: +50,000 for reaching Nowy Sącz.

9. Punctuality
- +10,000 bonus for arriving within a 60-second window.

------------------------------------------------------------------
C. TERMINAL PENALTIES (Triggers "Terminate = True")
------------------------------------------------------------------

10. Collision (Zone Overlap)
- Penalty: -100,000. Triggers if x_diff <= 0.

11. Safety Violation
- Small penalty (-10.0) for any violation logged by the VL.
- Episode may terminate based on violation type (handled in env).
